The Purpose of PAUSE

PAUSE is not about avoiding work or refusing to help. It is a tool for:

  • Prioritizing client care
  • Responding appropriately to urgent concerns
  • Managing time intentionally
  • Protecting scheduled clinical responsibilities
  • Distinguishing therapist duties from school duties
  • Setting and maintaining professional boundaries

A request can be important without requiring an immediate response from the therapist.
